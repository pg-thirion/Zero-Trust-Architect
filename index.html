Scoring model

Security contributes 75% of the final score. Business availability contributes 25%. Preventing threats yields the highest score, followed by containment and recovery.

A denied business-critical route reduces availability. An unenforced policy provides no protection. Results are deterministic to make architectural comparisons fair.
