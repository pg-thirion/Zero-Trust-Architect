      Zero trust glossary

      Identity and least privilege

      Verify each identity and grant only the access needed for its current role.

      Microsegmentation

      Separate network zones and enforce narrow, explicit communication policies.

      Continuous verification

      Re-evaluate device posture, identity and session risk throughout access.

      Defence in depth

      Combine prevention, detection, containment and recovery to reduce residual risk.

      Recovery and resilience

      Protect backups independently, test restores and preserve essential business services.
